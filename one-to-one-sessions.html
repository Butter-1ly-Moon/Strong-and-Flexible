How These Sessions Fit Into The Process

Many people arrive looking for a quick solution to pain or recurring injuries.

My approach is different.

Rather than treating each issue as an isolated problem, I focus on understanding how your body moves and identifying the factors that may be contributing to the issue in the first place.

The process typically follows three stages:

Movement Assessment

The assessment helps identify movement patterns, compensations and biomechanical habits that may be influencing how you move, perform and recover.

Movement Coaching & Development

One-to-One Sessions allow us to apply those findings through movement coaching, tactile cueing and practical movement strategies.

Long-Term Progress

Over time, sessions help improve movement awareness, build physical capacity and create more adaptable movement patterns that support long-term confidence and performance.

This integrated process helps ensure that every session has purpose and direction.

What Happens During A One-to-One Session?

Every session is guided by your goals, movement history and assessment findings. Depending on your needs, sessions may include a combination of the following:

Movement Coaching

Movement coaching helps improve awareness, coordination and control.

Using observation, guided movement and tactile feedback, I help clients better understand how their body responds to movement and load.

Biomechanics Integration

Biomechanics principles help us understand how force moves through the body and how different areas work together during movement.

This allows us to develop strategies that support more efficient movement and reduce unnecessary stress on the body.

Strength Development

Strength plays an important role in both performance and resilience.

Sessions may incorporate progressive loading, eccentric loading and other evidence-based Strength & Conditioning methods designed to improve physical capacity and movement confidence.

Mobility Development

Improving mobility is often about improving movement options rather than simply increasing flexibility.

Sessions may include targeted movement strategies that help develop greater freedom and control through movement.

Neuromuscular Re-Education

Many recurring issues are influenced by how the nervous system organises movement.

Through repetition, awareness and movement exploration, sessions help develop more effective movement habits over time.

Movement Retraining

This is where we address the 'Centipede's Dilemma.' When we place you into a biomechanically optimal position, your brain will initially sound an alarm because it feels 'unnatural.' My job is to guide you through that neurological friction until pain-free, efficient movement becomes your new automatic baseline.
