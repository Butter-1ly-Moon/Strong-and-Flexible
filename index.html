Personalised Movement Assessment & Rehabilitation in London

At Strong and Flexible, I combine over 10 years of practical experience with advanced movement methodologies including Gait Analysis and Anatomy in Motion (AiM) to assess how your body truly moves in real life, not just how symptoms appear in isolation.

My approach focuses on teaching movement awareness through intuitive tactile cueing, hands-on guidance, and evidence-based rehabilitation methods that help your body rediscover more efficient mechanics naturally.

Sessions are designed to help you:

  • Identify movement patterns contributing to pain, tension, or recurring injury
  • Improve joint control, coordination, and movement efficiency
  • Develop better proprioception and kinaesthetic awareness
  • Build strength and resilience through progressive loading strategies
  • Improve neuromuscular control and movement confidence
  • Create long-term, sustainable movement solutions rather than temporary symptom relief
  • Enhance physical performance, athletic movement quality, and long-term durability for active individuals and athletes

This approach is particularly valuable for individuals who feel they haven’t progressed with standard protocol-based rehabilitation, especially hypermobile or neurodivergent clients who often require more individualised movement coaching and tactile feedback.

A Modern, Evidence-Based Approach to Movement Rehabilitation

My work combines multiple evidence-based disciplines and rehabilitation methods, including:

  • Biomechanics & Gait Analysis
  • Anatomy in Motion (AiM)
  • Strength & Conditioning principles
  • Progressive and eccentric loading strategies
  • Neuromuscular re-education
  • Proprioception and movement awareness training
  • Overcoming isometrics
  • Movement coaching and tactile cueing techniques

Rather than relying on rigid protocols, I adapt sessions to the individual in front of me. Every person moves differently, learns differently, and responds differently to rehabilitation.

For many people, simply being shown exercises or given videos to follow is not enough. My approach uses tactile feedback and movement guidance to help your body learn movement patterns in a more intuitive and effective way.

Alongside rehabilitation, sessions can also support athletic development, movement optimisation, strength progression, injury prevention, and improved physical performance for active individuals across all levels of training and sport.
