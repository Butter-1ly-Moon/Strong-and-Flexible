Who I work with

My coaching brings together careful movement observation, hands-on guidance and progressive exercise. We explore how you move, what you can feel and control, and how you respond to different exercises. That helps us choose what to work on and how to progress.

Long-standing discomfort or movement restrictions

An old injury, recurring discomfort or a movement that never feels quite right may be getting in the way. We’ll explore your current abilities and limitations, then work on practical ways to develop strength, movement options and confidence.

Returning to exercise after injury

You want to return to the activities you enjoy, but feel unsure about where to start or how much to do. Sessions provide guided practice and gradual progression, starting with what you can manage now and working towards your goals.

Getting more from your training

You already run, lift, play sport or practise Pilates or yoga, and want focused help with mobility, coordination or a particular movement. My coaching can sit alongside your existing training, concentrating on the areas where you need individual attention.

A Modern, Evidence-Based Approach to Movement Rehabilitation

My work combines multiple evidence-based disciplines and rehabilitation methods, including:

  • Biomechanics & Gait Analysis
  • Anatomy in Motion (AiM)
  • Strength & Conditioning principles
  • Progressive and eccentric loading strategies
  • Neuromuscular re-education
  • Proprioception and movement awareness training
  • Overcoming isometrics
  • Movement coaching and tactile cueing techniques

Rather than relying on rigid protocols, I adapt sessions to the individual in front of me. Every person moves differently, learns differently, and responds differently to rehabilitation.

For many people, simply being shown exercises or given videos to follow is not enough. My approach uses tactile feedback and movement guidance to help your body learn movement patterns in a more intuitive and effective way.

Alongside rehabilitation, sessions can also support athletic development, movement optimisation, strength progression, injury prevention, and improved physical performance for active individuals across all levels of training and sport.
