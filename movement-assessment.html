What Is A Movement Assessment?

A Movement Assessment is a detailed evaluation of how your body moves during everyday activities, exercise and sport.

Rather than focusing solely on the area where symptoms appear, I assess how the entire body works together. In many cases, the place where pain or restriction occurs is not where the movement issue originates.

The assessment allows me to identify movement compensations, biomechanical inefficiencies, coordination challenges and loading strategies that may be influencing how you move, perform and recover.

Many clients arrive having spent months or even years trying different approaches without fully understanding why an issue keeps returning. A detailed assessment provides the clarity needed to move forward with confidence.

The information gathered during the assessment helps create a personalised roadmap for movement coaching, rehabilitation, performance development and long-term physical resilience.

What Happens During Your Movement Assessment?

A Movement Assessment is far more than a quick screening or basic posture check.

The purpose of the session is to understand how your body moves as a complete system, identify movement compensations, and uncover the factors that may be influencing recurring injuries, movement limitations, discomfort or performance challenges.

Every assessment is tailored to the individual because no two people move in exactly the same way.

1

Initial Consultation & Goal Setting

The session begins with a detailed discussion about your movement history, lifestyle, training background, previous injuries and current goals.

Whether you are looking to improve sporting performance, overcome recurring injuries, increase mobility or return to activities you enjoy, understanding the full picture helps guide the assessment process.

2

Gait Analysis

Walking is one of the most fundamental movement patterns we perform every day.

Through gait analysis, I assess how your body manages balance, rotation, weight transfer and propulsion. This often reveals movement habits and compensations that may influence both everyday movement and athletic performance.

3

Biomechanics Assessment

Next, I assess how different joints and body segments work together during movement.

Rather than examining isolated areas, I look at how the entire system coordinates movement, distributes force and adapts to physical demands.

This helps identify:

  • ✓ Movement compensations
  • ✓ Coordination challenges
  • ✓ Mobility restrictions
  • ✓ Load management issues
  • ✓ Inefficient movement strategies
4

Movement Testing & Exploration

Using movement coaching, observation and intuitive tactile cueing, I explore how your body responds to different movement tasks.

Many people learn movement more effectively through guided physical feedback than through verbal instruction alone. This stage often helps reveal movement opportunities that may have been overlooked previously.

5

Review Of Findings

Once the assessment is complete, I explain the findings in clear and practical language.

You will gain a better understanding of:

  • ✓ How your body currently moves
  • ✓ Where compensations may be occurring
  • ✓ Which movement patterns may be contributing to recurring issues
  • ✓ Areas where efficiency and performance may be improved
6

Personalised Next Steps

The final stage is creating a clear plan based on your assessment findings.

Depending on your goals, this may include:

  • ✓ Ongoing movement coaching
  • ✓ Biomechanics sessions
  • ✓ Root-cause rehabilitation
  • ✓ Mobility development
  • ✓ Strength and conditioning strategies
  • ✓ Performance-focused recommendations

The assessment provides the foundation for everything that follows, ensuring future sessions are based on your individual movement patterns rather than assumptions.
