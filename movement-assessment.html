Your first one-to-one session

Initial Movement Assessment & Practical Session

A first session to understand what you want to change, explore how you move and put useful ideas into practice.

60 minutes · £95 · Liverpool Street, London

What to expect

  1. 1. We talk about what matters to you

    We’ll discuss your goals, relevant injury or exercise history, and what you would like to get out of the session. That might be a movement you find difficult, a recurring restriction, or something you want to feel more confident doing. Together, we’ll agree a focus.

  2. 2. We explore how you move

    I’ll look at movements relevant to your concern. This may include walking, balance, mobility, strength or control. I’ll explain what I notice, how it relates to your goals and what remains uncertain.

  3. 3. We put ideas into practice

    You’ll try movements and exercises during the appointment, with individual coaching and, where useful and with your agreement, hands-on guidance. We’ll check how you respond and adjust what we do as we go.

  4. 4. We agree your next step

    We’ll leave time to bring the findings together, practise a small number of useful exercises and discuss what I recommend next. The aim is for you to leave with a clear, manageable starting point.

What happens afterwards?

A single session can be useful in its own right. You may prefer to practise independently, return for occasional reviews, or have regular coaching to develop and progress the work.

If I think further sessions would help, I’ll explain what they would add and why I recommend them. There’s no obligation to commit to regular appointments or a package.

You can also work with me alongside a trainer or clinician you already trust. If your concern would benefit from another professional’s assessment, we can discuss that too.
