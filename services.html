One-to-One Movement Coaching

Develop strength, mobility and control through individual coaching. Sessions combine practical exercises, hands-on guidance and feedback to help you understand and use your body more confidently—in everyday life, training or sport.

We work on the movements that matter to you, using floor exercises, reformer work or weights where useful. I’ll help you find positions and movements you can feel and control, then adjust the challenge as your ability develops.

Ongoing sessions give us time to revisit what you’ve practised, review your response and progress the work. You might choose regular coaching, occasional reviews or practical guidance to use alongside your existing training.

Returning clients: movement sessions are 60 minutes, £95. You can book individual appointments or explore the session packages.

Progressive Movement Rehabilitation

When discomfort or a movement difficulty keeps returning, we’ll explore your current abilities, restrictions and responses to different tasks. The aim is to find a useful starting point, then progressively develop the movement, strength and confidence needed for the activities that matter to you.

That can mean looking at how you walk, balance or manage load, including how different parts of your body work together. We’ll try exercises, observe what changes and use your feedback to decide what to practise and how to progress.

This work takes place within your movement coaching sessions. We can build from manageable movement tasks towards more demanding strength work and the activities you want to return to, alongside any care you already receive.

If another professional’s assessment would be helpful, I’ll explain why and discuss that with you.

Strength, Conditioning & Performance

Build the strength and physical capacity your activities demand. I work with recreational, competitive and professional athletes, including runners, ultra-endurance athletes and combat-sport practitioners.

Sessions can include weight training, controlled lowering movements, isometric holds and progressively challenging exercises. We’ll choose the movements, loads and pace around your current ability, goals and existing training.

The focus may be a particular movement, strength through a greater range, or building capacity for the demands of your sport. Mobility work includes developing strength and control in the ranges you need.

This is part of one-to-one movement coaching. We can work on specific areas alongside your coach or training programme, or develop a broader plan together.

Sports & Remedial Therapy

Focused hands-on treatment with a Level 5 qualified Sports & Remedial Massage Therapist. We begin with your goals and relevant movement checks, then select manual techniques to help ease discomfort and improve movement.

Depending on what we find and what feels appropriate for you, treatment may include soft tissue work, assisted movement, muscle energy techniques or joint mobilisation. We’ll check how you respond and adapt the session as we go.

This can suit people who already train regularly and want targeted treatment alongside their existing programme. Practical movement guidance may be included where helpful, without needing to commit to ongoing coaching.

How we choose what to work on

Movement assessment continues throughout our work together. I use movement observation, biomechanics and gait analysis where relevant to explore how you move, what you can feel and control, and how you respond to different exercises.

Hands-on guidance and tactile feedback can help make a movement easier to understand. Progressive exercise gives us a way to build on it. We’ll review what is useful for you and adjust the plan as your needs change.

You’ll work directly with me, Irek, at the studio near Liverpool Street. We can agree on practical work to continue between appointments and a pace of coaching that fits your goals.
