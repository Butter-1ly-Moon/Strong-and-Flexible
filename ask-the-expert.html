Ask the Expert: Making Sense of Movement

“I understand what you’re asking me to do, but why can’t I feel it?”

Questions like this come up regularly in my sessions. They are also the starting point for this section: practical explanations of movement, drawn from working with clients and exploring these challenges in my own body.

We’ll look at topics such as proprioception, your sense of body position and movement, how your feet respond to load, and why slowing an exercise down can reveal something you missed.

I use examples, diagrams and everyday comparisons to make technical ideas easier to explore. Where an explanation is a coaching analogy or reflects my own experience, I’ll make that clear.

The aim is to help you ask better questions, understand your movement more clearly and make more informed decisions about your practice and training.
