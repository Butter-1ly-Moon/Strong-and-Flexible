Movement package conditions

These conditions apply to the five- and ten-session movement packages.

Activation and completion: Your first package session must take place within 30 days of purchase. All sessions must take place within seven weeks of that first session. The initial assessment is separate.

Availability: Please check availability before purchasing and arrange your sessions early enough to complete your package.

Rescheduling and cancellations: Rescheduling is subject to availability and does not extend the completion deadline. Cancellations or changes with less than 24 hours’ notice count as a used session.

Exceptional circumstances: If exceptional circumstances interrupt your programme, please contact Irek promptly. Any extension must be agreed directly.

Online booking: Acuity may technically allow a booking beyond your package deadline. This does not extend the agreed validity period.

You will be asked to confirm that you have read and agree to the conditions for your selected package at checkout.
